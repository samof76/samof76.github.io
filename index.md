I live here with my wife, [daughter](https://instagram.com/dhartistry), and dog, while my son is currently studying abroad. I work at [Freshworks](https://freshworks.com) as a Principal Engineer, SRE. While I'm passionate about tech and open source, my interests extend beyond that—I'm deeply engaged with Dalit politics, movies, music, and art. These pages will contain my unfiltered and simple reflections on these experiences.
